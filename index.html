How the internet works – visualisation spikes

Four throwaway prototypes of the same mini-scene: phone → Wi-Fi access point → home router → internet, with animated packets. Tap (or pinch/scroll into) the Wi-Fi link to dive into radio waves, or the fibre link to see light pulses and DWDM colours. Switch language (EN / DA / AR) and kid/nerd mode in the top bar.

Spike 1

SVG + GSAP

Vanilla TypeScript, inline SVG, GSAP MotionPath for packets, d3-zoom for pan/pinch/smooth-zoom.

 Spike 2

Svelte 5 + SVG

Scenes as Svelte components rendering SVG from data; reactive i18n; Svelte transitions.

 Spike 3

PixiJS (WebGL 2D)

GPU canvas with bloom/glow filters, additive light and particles. Same SVG icons parsed into Pixi graphics.

 Spike 4

Three.js 2.5D

Tilted 3D map with bloom and camera dives; labels as DOM (CSS2DRenderer).

Deep links: every spike understands #/<lang>/<scene>, e.g. #/da/wifi or #/ar/fibre. Write-up and recommendation: docs/visualisation-spikes.md.
