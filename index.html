How the internet works – look & feel

The same little journey (phone → Wi-Fi → home router → fibre → the internet, and the video coming back) in four art styles. Tap a magnifier to look inside the Wi-Fi or the fibre, tap the cloud to unfold the internet, tap a parcel to follow it and peek at its envelopes, and flick or use ◀ ▶ to walk along the path. The flask button opens the lab with the interaction experiments (zoom transition, motion feel, lively packets, layout).

Storybook

A warm, flat picture book. Devices with faces, a little courier running with your parcel.

Neon blueprint

A dark sci-fi HUD: glowing wireframes, grid, scanlines and a targeting reticle.

Sketchbook

Dad's paper diagrams brought to life: wobbly pencil lines that boil, margin notes and hatching.

Paper cut-out

A layered craft-paper diorama with deep parallax between the layers.
